### How I work with AI

I wrote the first version of Umbrella by hand, deliberately, because I wanted to know exactly what was in the codebase before trusting a model with it. Now I use Claude daily for architecture review, refactors, and the parts of a build that are more typing than thinking, and I am the person both of my companies ask about where AI actually helps and where it does not. I have also worked with ChatGPT, Gemini, DeepSeek, and Kimi, and I run open models such as Llama locally through Ollama and across providers through OpenRouter.
